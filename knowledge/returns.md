# Return Policy

Customers may return eligible products within 30 days of the original delivery date.

The product must be unused and in its original packaging.

Customers should provide their order number when requesting a return.

Some products may be excluded from the standard return policy, including personalized products and final-sale items.

Customers can initiate a return by contacting customer support with their order number and reason for the return.

Once the returned item is received and inspected, the customer will receive confirmation that the return has been accepted or rejected.

Approved returns are generally refunded to the original payment method.
