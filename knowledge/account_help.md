# Account Help

Customers who have forgotten their password can use the password reset option on the account login page.

Password reset emails should arrive within several minutes.

Customers should check their spam or junk folder if they do not see the password reset email.

If the password reset email does not arrive after 15 minutes, the customer should contact support.

Customers should never provide their password to customer support representatives.

For account security issues, support may require verification of the customer's identity before making changes to the account.
