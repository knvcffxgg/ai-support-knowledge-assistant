# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping typically takes 3 to 5 business days after an order has been processed.

Orders are normally processed within 1 to 2 business days.

Customers receive a tracking number after their order has shipped.

If tracking information has not updated for more than 3 business days, the customer should contact support.

Expedited shipping may be available depending on the customer's location and the products ordered.

Shipping times may be longer during holidays, severe weather, or other periods of unusually high order volume.
