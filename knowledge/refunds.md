# Refund Policy

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method.

After a refund has been approved, it may take 5 to 10 business days for the funds to appear, depending on the customer's financial institution.

Customers should contact support if an approved refund has not appeared after 10 business days.

Refunds cannot normally be issued until the associated return has been received and inspected.

The final refund amount may depend on the condition of the returned product and the applicable return policy.
